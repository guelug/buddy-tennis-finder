Everything you need to look your best

Four powerful features working together to transform how you discover, try, and manage your personal style.

AI Stylist Chat

Chat with Luna, your personal AI stylist. Get outfit recommendations based on your wardrobe, preferences, and the occasion — powered by on-device Apple Intelligence.

Virtual Try-On

See how clothes look on you before buying. Upload a photo and use Gemini-powered AI to visualize outfits on your body with realistic lighting and fit.

AR Wardrobe

Place your clothes in augmented reality. Preview how items look in your space, mix and match layers, and build outfits in a virtual 3D environment.

Closet Management

Digitize your wardrobe with smart categorization, color tagging, and wear tracking. SwiftData keeps everything local, private, and instantly searchable.
